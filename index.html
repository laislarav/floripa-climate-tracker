Where does the data come from?

We use official data and published research, and we say how each figure was obtained. Because we cross several public databases, errors can happen.

Report an error

Emissions & mobility

  • Climate data: SEEG 13.0 and the 2022 inventory.
  • Mobility: +Pedal, Amobici and BNDES.
Technical details

Annual emissions come from SEEG 13.0; the chart uses gross emissions excluding land-use change, while net emissions include land-use emissions and removals. The 2022 city inventory is used as a cross-check, not mixed into the same trend. Cycling infrastructure comes from the city +Pedal programme; collision counts are preliminary Amobici data; spending shares come from the BNDES municipal finance analysis.

Neighborhood flood estimate

  • We cross the city GeoPortal risk map with 2022 population.
  • It is an areal estimate, not a door-to-door count.
Technical details

Flood-prone polygons and neighborhood boundaries come from the city GeoPortal. Residents in flood zones are estimated by overlaying the polygons on 2022 neighborhood population and assuming an even spread. It is an areal estimate, not a door-to-door count; use the GeoPortal for property-level checks.
